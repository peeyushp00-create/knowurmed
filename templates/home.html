Why Not Just Google It or Ask a Chatbot?

Both are useful tools — but neither was built for reviewing your actual medicines and prescription safely.

Google Search

Surfaces whatever's out there, unsorted by reliability. You have to judge each result yourself, and nothing is checked against your specific medicines together.

General AI Chatbots

Can sound confident while being wrong — general-purpose AI can hallucinate dosages or interactions, and has no particular obligation to protect a prescription photo you share with it.

KnowUrMed

Every fact traces to an admin-reviewed record with a source and review date. We show "Information unavailable" instead of guessing, present safety info as structured scannable cards instead of prose, and don't retain your prescription by default.

Why You Should Confirm With a Healthcare Professional

We Don't Diagnose or Prescribe

This tool explains and organizes information. It never diagnoses a condition or changes a prescription.

Data Has Limits

Our database may not cover every medicine, brand, or individual health situation.

OCR Can Be Wrong

Prescription handwriting and image quality can lead to misreads. Always double-check with your pharmacist.

Privacy and Data Protection

Your health information is sensitive, and we treat it that way.

  • Uploaded prescriptions are not stored permanently by default.
  • Sensitive profile data is never publicly visible.
  • You can view, edit, or delete your stored data at any time.
  • We ask for explicit consent before using your health profile for personalized checks.
